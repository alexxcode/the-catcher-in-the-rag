## Literary references

- The project name "The Catcher in the RAG" is a parody of *The Catcher in the Rye* (J.D. Salinger).
  No text, quotes, or imagery from that work appears in code, documentation, or UI.

- The `bartleby` component is named after the protagonist of *Bartleby, the Scrivener* (Herman Melville, 1853).
  This work is in the public domain. Short quotes from it may appear in code comments or documentation.
